Impact Categories

Environmental Impact

We increase tree cover, restore degraded land, retain natural vegetation, improve soil protection and build more connected and resilient landscapes. Each project begins with a baseline and defines measurable forestry, carbon and biodiversity indicators.

Economic Impact

We convert underutilised land into productive long-term assets. Revenue can be generated from sustainable timber, carbon, agroforestry products, honey and future biodiversity or ecosystem-service markets.

Community Impact

We create employment, contracting and outgrower opportunities while supporting transparent participation in carbon and produce value chains. Project structures are designed around clear rights, responsibilities and benefit sharing.

Governance & Accountability

We use documented land agreements, transparent financial structures, environmental and social safeguards, measurable baselines, independent technical inputs and auditable monitoring systems.
