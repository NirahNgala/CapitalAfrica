Operational

Koromi Farm Natural Capital Demonstration Site

Kilifi County, coastal Kenya

A dryland forestry, biodiversity and land-management site used to test commercial forestry, restoration, nursery systems, water management and operational approaches before larger-scale deployment.

Status Operational since 2019 · Established forestry blocks, conservation areas & ongoing trials

Objectives

  • Validate dryland forestry systems
  • Produce commercial timber assets
  • Conserve and restore natural vegetation
  • Test species and planting density
  • Develop operational capacity
  • Monitor biodiversity

Environmental Benefits

  • Increased tree cover & indigenous species
  • Habitat protection, soil cover & reduced erosion
  • Water-management improvements

Revenue Model

Long-term timber, poles, honey and potential carbon or biodiversity value. Primarily a proof-of-concept and operating platform.

In Development

SEKU Dryland Forestry & Natural Capital Partnership

South-eastern Kenya

A proposed long-term institutional land partnership combining dryland commercial forestry, carbon, agroforestry, restoration, research and local value addition.

Status Partnership concept under development · Subject to final agreement

Objectives

  • Develop a scalable nucleus forestry estate
  • Establish conservation corridors
  • Create commercial outgrower & smallholder programmes
  • Develop carbon assets
  • Build nursery and processing infrastructure

Environmental Benefits

  • Large-scale tree establishment
  • Restoration of degraded land
  • Improved soil cover, infiltration & habitat connectivity

Revenue Model

Carbon, poles, sustainable timber, moringa, honey and other compatible products. Detailed economics remain confidential.
