What We Do

From land opportunity to long-term natural capital asset.

1

Project Origination

We identify combinations of land, landowners, ecology, species, markets and financing that can support credible natural capital projects.

2

Project Development

Feasibility work, technical design, carbon screening, financial modelling, stakeholder agreements and investment preparation.

3

Implementation & Operations

Nursery production, land preparation, planting, maintenance, monitoring, logistics and long-term local management.

4

Natural Capital Value Creation

Multiple revenue streams including sustainable timber, carbon, biodiversity, agroforestry, honey and other compatible products.
