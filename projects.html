Operational Proof of Concept

Koromi Farm

Kilifi County, coastal Kenya

A dryland forestry, biodiversity and land-management site used to test commercial forestry, restoration, nursery systems, water management and operational approaches before larger-scale deployment.

Timeline

Established 2019 · Multi-rotation

Status

Operational with active trials

Focus

Proof-of-concept platform

Objectives

  • Validate dryland forestry systems
  • Produce commercial timber assets
  • Conserve and restore natural vegetation
  • Test species and planting density
  • Develop operational capacity
  • Monitor biodiversity

Environmental Benefits

  • Increased tree cover & indigenous species
  • Habitat protection and soil cover
  • Reduced erosion
  • Water-management improvements
  • Ongoing biodiversity monitoring

Revenue Model

Long-term timber, poles, honey and potential carbon or biodiversity value. Koromi is primarily a proof-of-concept and operating platform rather than a stand-alone investment offering.

Current Status

Operational, with established forestry blocks, conservation areas, water infrastructure and ongoing trials.

Detailed project information, maps, commercial assumptions and capital requirements are available to qualified partners.

In Development

SEKU Dryland Forestry Partnership

South-eastern Kenya

A proposed long-term institutional land partnership combining dryland commercial forestry, carbon, agroforestry, restoration, research and local value addition.

Stage

Subject to final agreement

Focus

Nucleus estate + outgrowers

Products

Carbon, timber, moringa, honey

Objectives

  • Develop a scalable nucleus forestry estate
  • Establish conservation corridors
  • Create commercial outgrower & smallholder programmes
  • Develop carbon assets
  • Build nursery and processing infrastructure

Environmental Benefits

  • Large-scale tree establishment
  • Restoration of degraded land
  • Improved soil cover and infiltration
  • Habitat connectivity
  • Reduced pressure on natural forests

Revenue Model

Carbon, poles, sustainable timber, moringa, honey and other compatible products. Detailed economics remain confidential.

Current Status

Partnership concept under development. Public wording must be approved by SEKU before launch.

Detailed project information is available to qualified partners following an introductory discussion and confidentiality agreement.
